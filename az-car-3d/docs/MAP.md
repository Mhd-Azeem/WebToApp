# Hybrid Sri Lankan map

This is an original compressed fictional district, not a surveyed recreation.
Colombo is the playable focus: a roughly 340 × 360 metre connected district,
Fort/Pettah-style shop blocks, balconies/awnings, crossings, curbs, street lights,
Galle Face-inspired coastal promenade and sea wall. All road, building, curb and
boundary collision uses simple static shapes. Roads are approximately 15 m wide.

The eastern accessible loop at x=160 is a rural paddy-road fringe. Low-rise
arcades reference Galle's colonial streets. A northern hill skyline references
Kandy. These establish the hybrid direction; full Kandy/Galle/village districts,
hill roads and intercity highways remain expansion work, with signs indicating
those future connections. Region references are not claims of complete cities.

Forward vehicle axis is local +Z. Spawn faces -Z, on the -X half of
the road: the driver's left lane. Camera/steering wheel sit at local -X,
which is the vehicle's right side. Give-way signs and crossings show conventions;
no autonomous traffic or enforcement system is included yet.

Next art pass: licensed medium-detail sedan, modular facade variations, baked
ambient occlusion, textured vegetation and bespoke Colombo landmarks. The current
car and palms are deliberately simple original models. Textured buildings still
use modular box-based meshes; there are no photogrammetry buildings or interiors.
